Herein are the rules set forth by your fearless leaders Brown and Vest. Read them, understand them, feel them.

Keepers Rules
  • You may keep 3 players of any position
  • Keepers must be declared 1 week before the draft
  • Keepers will cost you a draft pick based on the pick you used to select them the previous year
      • For every additional year you keep a player after the first year, you will lose the round you selected them + 1 round per year
      Example: If you drafted a player in the 5th round last year, and you are keeping them for a second year, you will lose your 6th round pick this year
      If you keep a player in the first round, you will lose a first and fifth round pick the next year
      The same incremental year increase applies to these players
      Example: If you keep a player drafted in the first round for two years, you will lose your first and fourth round pick
  • You may only keep players if you have the picks to keep them
  • If you trade away a pick that you would have used to keep a player, you forfeit the right to keep that player
      • Example: If you want to keep two first round players, you must have two first round picks, a fifth round pick, and a fourth round pick
